## Tips when MC-ing at a wedding

- Stick to the schedulue
- Make sur people have prepared their speeches
  - Make sure it's not too long
- Test all videos with sound before
  - Might be good to add them to youtube
- Have single "keynote" with all information and all content if possible
  - Minimises the need to change between presentations
- Add the "Welcome" slide between presentations
  - For a nice transition when no one is speaking
